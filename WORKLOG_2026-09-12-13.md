## Still open

**The backend has not been deployed.** Around ten fixes are written, tested and
sitting in the repository — including the websocket leak, where any signed-in
customer could subscribe to another customer's live readings using a device id,
and the roles screen, which returned other customers' names and email addresses.
Neither is closed in production until the backend ships.

**Storage is the next deadline.** 275 MB of 512 MB used, growing about
18 MB a day — roughly **8 days**. The 15-day retention rule starts expiring the
oldest readings around 23 September, which may level it off, but that is close
to the ceiling. The choice is a shorter retention period or a larger plan
(~US$57/month).

**Tests run against the live database.** This is why test accounts keep
appearing on the SFTP server — the provisioning agent picks up jobs the tests
create and builds real Unix accounts. Cleaned up three times now. A separate
test database is the fix; everything else is mopping.
